# Philippine Marketplace Produce Master List

This is the working source list for future catalog products and image generation. It intentionally
keeps both aggregate products and named varieties, so `Banana` and `Banana Cavendish`, for example,
can become separate sellable catalog items.

## Scope

- 142 vegetable and root-crop entries tracked by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
- 83 fruit entries tracked by the PSA
- 1 requested retail variant not separated in the PSA table: Japanese cucumber
- 226 total candidate catalog entries

The PSA tables include vegetables, roots, herbs, legumes, edible flowers, shoots, leaves, pith, and
some aggregate/subtype rows. Those distinctions are preserved here for catalog planning. Check an
item after its product record and final image are ready.

Sources:

- [PSA Vegetables and Root Crops table](https://openstat.psa.gov.ph/PXWeb/pxweb/en/DB/DB__2E__CS/0082E4EVCP3.px/)
- [PSA Fruit Crops table](https://openstat.psa.gov.ph/PXWeb/pxweb/en/DB/DB__2E__CS/0072E4EVCP2.px/)
- [DOST-PCAARRD Philippine Indigenous Vegetables](https://iveg.pcaarrd.dost.gov.ph/crop)

## Image-generation conventions

- Use one isolated product per image on a genuinely transparent background.
- Use consistent soft studio lighting, natural color, and realistic texture.
- Use a centered three-quarter arrangement with enough padding for square product cards.
- Show the commonly purchased amount: one bunch, one pack, or a small group rather than an entire
  harvest.
- Use separate images for parent products and visibly distinct named varieties.
- Do not add labels, packaging, logos, decorative props, or watermarks.
- Save final catalog assets as optimized square WebP files under
  `apps/web/public/marketplace/`.

## Generated asset coverage

- 226 transparent WebP assets are generated under `apps/web/public/marketplace/produce/`.
- Every asset is 512×512 pixels with an alpha channel; the complete set is approximately 11.1 MB.
- Filenames use lowercase kebab-case slugs matching the checklist names.
